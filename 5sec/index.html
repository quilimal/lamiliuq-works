How it works

Three steps. That's all.

5sec. is a camera app that captures five seconds from your life — not a message, just the feeling of being there.

01

Set up notifications

5sec. will occasionally remind you to capture a moment — but only during the hours you allow. No daily pressure, no streaks to keep.

02

Tap, and it's already recording

One tap starts a five-second recording instantly. Tap to focus, pinch to zoom. Swipe left or right to switch between five built-in looks — Normal, Food, Landscape, Portrait, and Night — free, right out of the box. When it's done, the share sheet opens — post it, or close the sheet and it's gone.

03

Go PRO for more

Unlock three more looks — Monochrome, VHS, and Broken Camera — right in the same swipe. Add your own frame to make every scene unmistakably yours.

📡 Data Transmission

5sec. does not operate its own servers. The videos you record are never sent to any server managed by us.

However, communication with external services may occur in the following cases, within the scope necessary for the respective functionality:

  • App update notifications and version compatibility checks
  • In-app purchase processing
  • Integration with Apple-provided features, including the App Store
  • Sharing a recording to social media or messaging apps, which you initiate yourself

We do not collect or access your recordings through any of these communications.

✓ We never send what you record to our servers.

📱 Data Storage

All data used by this app — including your settings and preferences — is stored exclusively on your iPhone using Apple's standard local storage. Recordings are only kept on your device until you record a new one or close the app.

No data is automatically transmitted to external services.

✓ Your data stays on your device.
